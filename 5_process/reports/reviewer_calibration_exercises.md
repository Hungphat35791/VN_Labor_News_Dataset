# Reviewer Calibration Exercises

**Exercise set version:** `1.0.0`  
**Purpose:** Independent reviewer practice using the current official `labeling_guideline.md`.  
**Status:** Synthetic scenarios only. These are not dataset records, official annotations, or ground truth.

## Instructions for reviewers

- The coordinator should provide this exercise document and only the recipient's own blank calibration sheet.
- Work independently. Do not discuss answers with the other reviewer or consult any answer key until both reviewers have submitted their sheets.
- For each scenario, decide on your own whether the information supports one of `Relevant`, `Borderline`, `Not relevant`, or `Uncertain`. Use one topic A–L only when required by the official guideline.
- Write the evidence and reasoning in your own words. If the guideline does not resolve a case, state what is unclear; do not invent a rule.
- These exercises are separate from the 620-article master sample and must never be copied into production annotation data.

## Scenarios

### CAL-01

Two employers in a Vietnamese industrial park report current vacancies and say they are unable to fill several positions. The article compares the number and types of open jobs at the two employers.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and, if required, one primary topic. Explain which article facts support your decision.

### CAL-02

A report follows several employees who submit voluntary resignation notices. It focuses on their notice periods, the end of their employment, and the employers' process for acknowledging the resignations. It does not report layoffs or a broader change in labor supply.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and, if required, one primary topic. Explain the distinction that matters for your decision.

### CAL-03

A factory announces an involuntary staff reduction. The article gives equal prominence to the number of jobs being cut and to workers' unpaid wages. Neither subject is described as secondary, and the article provides no further indication of which issue is its main focus.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and, if required, one primary topic. If the current guideline does not resolve the primary-topic choice, identify the competing interpretations and the clarification needed.

### CAL-04

A company closes a Vietnamese production site and informs its employees that their positions will end because of the closure. The article focuses on the workers who will lose their jobs and the timing of the workforce reduction.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and, if required, one primary topic. Explain the nature of the employment event.

### CAL-05

A report describes an accident during a work shift at an active construction site. It details the hazards workers faced and the safety inspections conducted at the site after the accident.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and, if required, one primary topic. Identify the connection between the event and the work described.

### CAL-06

Employees at a workplace stop work over a disputed workplace arrangement. Their elected worker representatives meet management to negotiate a resolution, and the article focuses on those negotiations.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and, if required, one primary topic. Explain what makes the reported activity substantive.

### CAL-07

People in Vietnam are recruited with false overseas job offers. After arriving abroad, they are compelled to work under threats and are prevented from leaving. The report focuses on the recruitment deception and the workers' treatment.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and, if required, one primary topic. Explain the labor connection supported by the scenario.

### CAL-08

A Vietnamese public program offers vocational retraining and job-placement assistance to workers affected by a change in an industry. The article explains the program's eligibility and employment-support measures.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and, if required, one primary topic. Base your decision on the program described.

### CAL-09

A courier is attacked while carrying out a delivery. The report describes this one event but says nothing about workplace safety arrangements, prevention measures, or broader working conditions.

**Your task:** Decide whether the available facts are enough to assign a relevance label and primary topic under the current guideline. If not, specify what human clarification or adjudication is needed.

### CAL-10

A crime report mentions that the person affected works as a courier. The article explicitly establishes that the event was unrelated to the person's work, duties, workplace, employment, or worker rights.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and indicate whether a topic should be recorded. Explain which fact determines your decision.

### CAL-11

An article says a proposed industrial park may create jobs in the future. It provides no confirmed vacancies, hiring plans, or information about an actual workforce.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and, if required, one primary topic. Explain how the article's level of certainty affects your decision.

### CAL-12

The available text is truncated and contains conflicting descriptions of whether the person involved was carrying out assigned work or was engaged in a private activity. The missing portion may resolve the connection.

**Your task:** Choose a relevance label and indicate whether a topic should be recorded. State exactly what evidence is missing or contradictory.

## Submission

Enter decisions only in the blank calibration sheet assigned to you. Use the same `scenario_id` shown above. Leave answers unresolved where the guideline requires clarification; do not use these practice responses as labels for real articles.
